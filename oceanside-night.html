Fish Shop Oceanside

236 S Coast Hwy, Oceanside, CA 92054

🚕 ~5 min ride from home · ~8 min walk to the Rooftop · Sat 11 AM–9 PM (their site)

Super casual, fun seafood spot that opened in 2026. Pick your fish at the case, then have it as a taco, sandwich, salad or plate. Full bar and a patio. Flip-flops fine.

💲 Example: mahi plate $26.25 + jumbo shrimp plate $32.50 = $58.75 (their menu) + 2 drinks est. $16 = ~$75, + ~8% tax $6 + ~10% tip $8 ≈ ~$90 for two (estimate). 📅 No reservations: order at the counter. Arrive by ~7 since the kitchen closes at 9.
