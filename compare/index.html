Oasis by the Sea, Carlsbad

$2,300 · about $2,480 with gas

Pros

  • About $418 cheaper and within the ~$2,500 budget
  • Top-floor oceanfront corner unit, white-water views, sunset deck

  • Carlsbad Village restaurants and coffee a 2-minute walk

Cons

  • No air conditioning (fine in mid-October)

Pros
