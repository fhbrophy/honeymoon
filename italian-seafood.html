Italian seafood dinner near the Oceanside Strand, ranked by value

Frank & Brielle · one fancy dinner, Thu Oct 15 – Sun Oct 18, 2026 · ~7:30–8 PM seating after sunset (~6:15 PM). Totals are for two with a drink each, ~8% tax (estimate) and 20% tip. Drink prices (est. $15 each) and drive times are estimates.

Verdict: The best pick is Vigilucci's Seafood & Steakhouse. It costs about $20 more than Vigilucci's Cucina (~$182 vs ~$161) and adds an oceanfront patio and room, which is the most romantic setting on this list. Verise is the wildcard: new and chef-driven, but its prices aren't confirmed. North County has no Michelin-recognized Italian seafood restaurant in range.

Most romantic

Vigilucci's Seafood & Steakhouse – Carlsbad

View menuRead reviews
~$182 for two ($66 + $46 + est. $30 drinks = $142, + ~8% tax $11 + 20% tip $28)

Italian seafood and prime steaks, à la carte, across from the ocean. Heated oceanfront patio and a panoramic Veranda room.

Try: Cioppino ($66), Fettuccine Salmone e Gamberetti ($46), from their menu.

Drive: ~12 min · Open: Thu–Sun (Fri/Sat to 10)

Caveat: The ocean is dark at 7:30, so ask for a heated patio table to hear the waves. No critic coverage found.
