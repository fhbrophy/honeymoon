Fancy dinner

Valle – Oceanside (5–8 min) · $220/person + 20% service; ~$930+ for two with pairing
Michelin-listed Baja tasting menu with a fireplace, overlooking the pier. Open Thu–Sat. Top pick, the closest.
Jeune et Jolie – Carlsbad (~15 min) · $120/person for 4 courses; ~$350–450 for two
French food on a heated garden patio under string lights. Michelin star confirmed for 2023–24 only. Open all four nights, bookable on OpenTable.
Lilo – Carlsbad (~15 min) · ~$265/person
Michelin-starred tasting counter with 24 seats. Closed Sundays.
Campfire – Carlsbad (~15 min) · est. $200–300 for two
Wood-fired cooking, the most relaxed of the four. Open Sundays.
