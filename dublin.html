ℹ️ Know before you go

🚋 Getting around: Compact and walkable. Leap card: TFI 90-minute fare €2 adult Zone 1 across Dublin Bus, Luas (trams: Red line = Smithfield/Docklands/Heuston; Green = Stephen's Green/O'Connell) and DART (coast: Howth, Malahide, Dún Laoghaire). Leap Visitor Card: €8/24 h, €18/72 h, €24/7 days (~$9/~$20/~$27). Airport: Dublin Express from €9 online or Aircoach 700 ~€8 (~$10). Taxis: FreeNow is the main hailing app; Uber works but books licensed taxis (metered fares).
🌧️ Mid-Nov weather & light: Sunrise ~7:53 AM, sunset ~4:25 PM (Nov 17) — ~8.5 hrs daylight, so outdoor day-trip walks must start by early afternoon. Expect cool (~high 40s–low 50s °F, typical-climate estimate), damp and windy; bring a rain shell and layers. Kilmainham is very cold inside.
💱 Prices: USD at €1 = $1.117 (ECB reference rate, Oct 30, 2026), rounded. Badges: ✔ own site checked hours/price read on the official site today; ◐ partly verified some details from search/third-party; ✖ unverified confirm before going. Hours checked Oct 31, 2026 — re-check the week of travel.
